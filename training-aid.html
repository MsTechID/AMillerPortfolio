Training Aid

Before you build training… ask… is a one-page decision tree for diagnosing a performance problem before assuming training is the answer. It walks through whether expectations are clear, whether the skill was ever performed correctly, whether the tools and environment support it, and whether consequences and incentives are aligned. Each answer routes to a training solution, a non-training solution, or a prompt to dig deeper. Color-coded so the recommendation is readable at a glance.
